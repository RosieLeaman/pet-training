# Pet Training plugin
This plugin lets your pets learn from watching you train skills and level up their skills too.

Have you ever looked at your pet while you're mining and wondered what they are thinking?
They have been learning from you! Now with this plugin you can track how much they've learnt.

# How it works
If you have a pet out any time you get exp in a skill, that same exp is given to your pet.
They learn from being there while you gain exp :)
They can learn from any skill training method irrespective of their skill level, as they have you as a great teacher.

If you have more than one pet, you can also check the exp of your other pets in the panel by selecting another in the drop-down menu.

# Known issues
Not fully tested but I expect that it will not work properly with cats/dogs when they grow from kitten/puppy to cat or become a hellcat.
